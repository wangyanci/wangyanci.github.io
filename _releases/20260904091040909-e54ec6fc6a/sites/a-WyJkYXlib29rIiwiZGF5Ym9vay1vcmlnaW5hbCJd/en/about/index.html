This is a static blog and the personal knowledge base where I publish my Obsidian notes.

I built this site to give my notes a dedicated home.

I learn by reading, taking systems apart, and reproducing excellent projects. Obsidian is where I keep those notes, and this site is where I publish them.

The site preserves Obsidian features such as wikilinks, the knowledge graph, callouts, and embedded blocks so the notes retain their original organization and reading experience on the web.

The material here may change as my understanding improves. I hope one of these notes happens to help you.
